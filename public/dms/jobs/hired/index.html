10 job hacks to copy-paste

From finding jobs other people miss to asking for a better salary. Tap Copy, paste it where it says, and swap the [brackets] for your own details.

  1. #01FIND

    Hidden jobs

    Search → paste into Google

    SEARCH ↓
    site:jobs.lever.co "data analyst" Bengaluru

    Many companies post jobs on their own careers page (Lever) before or instead of job sites. Change the role and city. Try the same search with site:job-boards.greenhouse.io and site:jobs.ashbyhq.com. Then Tools → Past week, to see only new jobs.

  2. #02FIND

    Startup jobs

    Search → paste into Google

    SEARCH ↓
    site:keka.com careers fresher Bengaluru

    Lots of Indian startups run their careers pages on Keka. This finds their fresher and intern openings directly. Change "fresher" to your role, or the city to yours.

  3. #03FIND

    Hiring posts

    Search → paste into LinkedIn

    SEARCH ↓
    "hiring" "data analyst" Pune

    Paste it into LinkedIn search, tap Posts, then sort by Latest. People post "We're hiring" before the job is listed. Reply or DM the person who posted, with one line on why you fit.

  4. #04FIND

    Daily job alert

    Prompt → paste into ChatGPT

    PROMPT ↓
    Every morning, search the web for 5 new [role] jobs in [city] posted in the last 24 hours. For each one give me the company, the role, the experience needed and the link to apply. Skip anything older than 24 hours.

    ChatGPT turns this into a scheduled task and sends you the list every day. The free plan allows up to 3 active scheduled tasks, run at most once a day.

  5. #05APPLY

    Recruiter test

    Prompt → paste into Claude

    PROMPT ↓
    You are a recruiter hiring for the job below, and you have 7 seconds to look at my resume. 1) What do you notice first? 2) Give me the top 3 reasons you would reject me. 3) For each reason, tell me the exact line to change.

Job post: [paste it here]

(My resume is attached.)

    Attach your resume as a PDF and paste the job post. Fix the 3 reasons, then run it again until there is nothing big left.

  6. #06APPLY

    Referral message

    Prompt → paste into ChatGPT

    PROMPT ↓
    Write a 3-line LinkedIn message to [name], [their role] at [company]. I am applying for [job title]: [job link]. Mention this from their profile: [one thing you noticed]. Ask if they would be open to referring me. Friendly, no begging, under 300 characters.

    Under 300 characters so it also fits in a LinkedIn connection note. Edit it so it sounds like you, then send.

  7. #07APPLY

    Learn the missing skill

    Prompt → paste into Cotsi

    PROMPT ↓
    /cotsi teach me [the skill from the job post]. My interview is in [number] days.

    Cotsi is a tutor I built. It teaches the few ideas a skill depends on first, asks you a question after each one, and brings it back for review so it sticks.

  8. #08INTERVIEW

    Company homework

    Prompt → paste into Perplexity

    PROMPT ↓
    Find [company]'s most important news from the last 3 months: launches, funding, hiring, problems. Then give me 3 smart questions I can ask in my interview for [role] that show I did my homework.

    Perplexity searches the web and shows its sources. Open one or two of them, so you can talk about the news, not just quote it.

  9. #09INTERVIEW

    Mock interview

    Prompt → paste into Gemini Live

    PROMPT ↓
    Interview me for [role] at [company]. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Be strict. After each answer, score me out of 10 and tell me one thing to improve. Start with "Tell me about yourself".

    In the Gemini app, tap Live and answer out loud, like a real interview. Saying it out loud is the practice. ChatGPT's voice mode works the same way.

  10. #10OFFER

    Salary ask

    Prompt → paste into Claude

    PROMPT ↓
    I got an offer of Rs [X] LPA for [role] in [city], with [years] years of experience. Give me one polite line to ask for more, and what to say if they say the budget is fixed. Short and confident.

    Ask before you accept, not after. Get the final number in writing.

Start with #1 and #5 today. Find 5 jobs nobody else is looking at, then make sure your resume survives the recruiter's 7 seconds.
