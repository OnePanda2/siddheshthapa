Find a business idea AI can't

AI only knows what someone already wrote down. The best ideas are in what people say to a friend and never post: their real problems. Here is how to hear them in 10 short conversations.

1Who to talk to

  • People who do one job every dayA shopkeeper, a tuition teacher, a delivery rider, a clinic receptionist. Ask about their day, not about your idea.
  • People who already pay for a fixSomeone paying a person, an app or a service to handle something. Money spent today is the strongest clue there is.
  • People around one placeYour street, your college, your society. Ask what is missing there. AI cannot see your gali.

Ten people, 10 to 15 minutes each. In person or on a call. Do not survey; talk.

2The 7 questions

  1. "Tell me about the last time you dealt with [the problem]."

    A real story from the past. Opinions about the future are guesses; past stories are facts.

  2. "What was the hardest part?"

    Let them name the pain in their own words. Write those words down exactly.

  3. "How do you handle it today?"

    Their workaround: an app, a helper, a notebook, WhatsApp, or nothing.

  4. "What does that cost you, in time or money?"

    Hours a week, rupees a month, a person they pay. This tells you if it is worth solving.

  5. "What have you already tried to fix it? Why did it not work?"

    If they tried nothing, the problem may be small. If they tried three things, it is real.

  6. "From 1 to 10, how well does your way work today?"

    A 5 or 6 means room for something better. A 9 means leave it alone.

  7. "Who else should I talk to about this?"

    One conversation turns into the next. Ten people becomes easy.

No problem in mind yet? Start with: "What is the most annoying part of your week?" and follow it with these.

3Signs you found something

  • The same complaint 3 or more times from people who do not know each other.
  • They already spend money or hours on it a paid helper, an app, a daily workaround.
  • They rate today's way 6 or lower and they lean in when you describe a fix.
  • They ask "can you do it for me?" Do it by hand first, for free. If it helps, charge the next person.

4Mistakes that hide the truth

  • Do not ask "Would you use my app?" People say yes to be nice.
  • Do not pitch your idea until the last minute. Listen first.
  • Do not argue with a low rating. Write it down.
  • "That's interesting" is not demand. Money, time or a promise is.

After each talk, write 3 lines

1. Their problem, in their exact words.

2. What it costs them today (time or money).

3. Their 1 to 10 rating of today's way.

After 10 talks, read your notes. The idea is the problem that keeps coming back.
