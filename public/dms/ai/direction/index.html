The Direction template

Context tells AI why. Direction tells it exactly what. AI can't see the picture in your head; it only sees your words, and every gap you leave gets the most average choice. Tap Copy, swap the [brackets] for your own words, and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI.

  • MOVE 1Say what "done" looks like: how long, what format, who it's for, where it's used.
  • MOVE 2Show, don't describe. One example beats ten adjectives.
  • MOVE 3Say what must not change. Change nothing else.
  • MOVE 4Never say "make it better". Say what's wrong and what right looks like.
  • SHORTCUTLet the AI ask you first.
  1. #01THE TEMPLATE

    Context + all four moves

    Use it at the start of anything that's yours: a video, a design, an email, a plan. Swap every [bracket] for your own words. Skip a line only if it really doesn't apply.

    PROMPT ↓
    CONTEXT (why)
I'm making [what you're making] for [who it's for], because [why it matters].

MOVE 1: what "done" looks like
It should be [how long], as [what format], for [who will use it], used on [where].

MOVE 2: an example
Here's an example of what I mean: [paste an example, or attach a screenshot].

MOVE 3: what must not change
Keep [what must stay the same]. Don't add [what you don't want].

THE SHORTCUT
Feel free to ask me anything before you start. Don't leave any room for assumptions.
  2. #02MOVE 4

    When the result is wrong

    Never reply "make it better". Better how? Decide what exactly is wrong and what the right version looks like, then say both.

    PROMPT ↓
    What's wrong: [exactly what is wrong].
What the right version looks like: [describe it].
Change only that. Change nothing else.
  3. #03MOVE 2

    Can't find the words? Screenshot it

    Screenshot a design you love. Send it with step 1. When the AI has described it, send step 2. Now you have the exact words you couldn't find.

    STEP 1 ↓
    Describe everything you see in this image: the layout, colors, fonts, sizes, spacing, and the mood. Be as detailed as you can.
    STEP 2 ↓
    Turn that description into one prompt I can use to make the same design.
  4. #04THE SHORTCUT

    Let the AI ask you

    Add this to the end of any prompt. Every guess the AI was about to make turns into a question, and answering a question is easier than describing the picture in your head.

    ADD TO ANY PROMPT ↓
    Feel free to ask me anything. Don't leave any room for assumptions.

Start with #01 on the next thing you make. Then, when the result is wrong, reply with #02 instead of "make it better".
