6 FREE study AI tools

The ones most students have not heard of. Every link goes to the tool's own site. Free plans were checked on 14 Nov 2026; limits can change, so check the site.

01Napkin AI FREE

Paste your notes and get a diagram or flowchart in seconds.

Free forever: 500 AI credits a week, PNG and PDF export (with a small Napkin mark).

Try it: Paste one paragraph of your notes, then click the spark icon next to it.

Open Napkin AI →

02Turbo AI FREE

Drop in a lecture recording or a PDF and get notes, flashcards and a quiz.

Free tier includes note generation, flashcards and quizzes (with monthly limits).

Try it: Upload one chapter PDF and press generate.

Open Turbo AI →

03ElevenReader FREE

Listen to any PDF or article in a natural, human-like voice.

Free app (Android and iOS) with free listening hours every month.

Try it: Install the app, import a PDF, press play.

Open ElevenReader →

04Goblin Tools FREE

Type a big task like "exam ki taiyari" and Magic ToDo breaks it into small steps.

Free, no account needed.

Try it: Open Magic ToDo, type your task, tap the magic wand.

Open Goblin Tools →

05Google AI Studio FREE

Upload a whole book as a PDF and ask it anything about it.

Free with a Google account (usage limits apply).

Try it: Add your PDF with the + button, then ask: "Chapter 7 ka summary do".

Open Google AI Studio →

06 · mine

Cotsi: your favourite character teaches you

Tell Cotsi a topic, and it teaches you the 20% that matters first, in the style of your favourite movie or TV character. It is a free Claude skill: a free Claude account can add it under Customize → Skills.

Launching 16 November. The link goes here on launch day.
