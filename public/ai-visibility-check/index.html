Why great clinics get skipped

AI recommends what it can read and verify.

01

It reads directories, not just your website

Answers lean on the profiles AI trusts in your field and city: Psychology Today, BACP, Medimap, WhatClinic, Doctify, nhs.uk. Missing or half-filled profiles mean you aren't in the running.

02

Your site doesn't say who, what and where

Many clinic sites never state the city in the title, have no machine-readable business details, or hide their text behind scripts that AI crawlers can't read.

03

Reviews alone won't save you

Many regulated professionals can't ask clients for reviews or use testimonials. So the signals you can control matter even more.

Questions

Before you ask

Is this just SEO with a new name?

It builds on the same basics, but the target is different. Google shows ten links; an AI assistant names a few practices and quotes the directories and facts it trusts. We work on those sources directly and measure whether you're named.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No, and be wary of anyone who says they can. AI answers change from one run to the next. We guarantee the setup work within 14 days and measure how often you're named before and after.

What do you need from us?

About 20 minutes: manager access to your Google Business Profile, a few practice details, and either access to your website or a contact who updates it. You approve every word before it goes live.

Do you work with NHS GP surgeries?

No. Patients register with an NHS GP by catchment area on nhs.uk, so visibility work doesn't help them. We work with private clinics and with dentists who offer NHS and private care.

You're in India. How does that work?

All the work is online: listings, profiles, website changes and AI testing. Calls are on Google Meet at a time that suits your clinic, UK or Canadian business hours.
