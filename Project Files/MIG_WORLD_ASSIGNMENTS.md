## LOCKED — the three reference implementations

### PHILOSOPHY
- **World type** planetary (compact, neural)
- **Template** TRAPPIST-1 · **Source** NASA Exoplanet Archive, pscomppars
- **Why** 7 concepts ↔ 7 measured planets, exact. The densest, most
  interconnected region gets the most tightly nested real system.
- **Topology** 21 objects, 14 internal relationships, 8 components
- **Visual personality** neural central star with branching dendrites; seven
  packed orbits inside a 5.36× span
- **Colour** violet / electric blue
- **Motion** still
- **Status** LOCKED

### LOVE
- **World type** circumbinary
- **Template** Kepler-16 · **Source** NASA archive + Doyle et al. 2011 (Science 333, 1602)
- **Why** its own content is dual — ATTACHMENT ↔ DISTANCE, and a contradiction
  reading *INDEPENDENCE vs BEING KNOWN*. Two stellar centres, a measured 3.15×
  hollow middle.
- **Topology** 7 objects, 4 internal relationships, 1 isolate
- **Visual personality** two unequal stars swinging anti-phase about an empty
  barycentre; a dashed forbidden zone; few far bodies
- **Colour** warm amber / rose
- **Motion** the pair swings while the camera travels
- **Status** LOCKED

### OBSERVATION
- **World type** constellation (sparse)
- **Template** Ursa Major (Big Dipper) + Alcor · **Source** SIMBAD, CDS Strasbourg
- **Why** the only MIG whose objects form a single connected chain — 7 in a line
  plus exactly one isolate (ANOMALY → Alcor, the eyesight test). Its PATTERNS
  concept warns that seeing a pattern is *a source of error*; a constellation is
  that warning made geometry.
- **Topology** 8 objects, 6 internal relationships, chain + 1 isolate
- **Visual personality** real star positions in true 3D; the mind's own six
  relationships as the figure; 42 real background stars
- **Colour** verdigris / cold teal, with cold gold reserved for the anomaly
- **Motion** parallax — the figure comes apart when you move off its line of sight
- **Status** LOCKED

**Honest count: 8 of 15 are planetary variants.** Not a default — 11 of the 12
verified systems on hand are exoplanetary. They differ substantially (spreads
1.68× to 362.7×; 2 to 7 bodies; resonant vs graded vs stratified) but share a
renderer. Reducing that concentration properly needs data not yet researched:
**open star clusters** (many bodies, no centre), **nebulae** (honest astronomy
for latent regions), and the three unsourced constellations above.

## Open decisions

1. **BUSINESS ↔ 55 Cnc** — 7 concepts against 5 usable orbits. HD 219134
   (compact core + one far-flung outlier) is the alternative.
2. **FOOD ↔ TOI-178 vs HUMAN BEHAVIOUR ↔ GJ 876** — both resonant. If they read
   as the same world in implementation, one must move.
3. **Whether MUSIC and PSYCHOLOGY stay visually empty**, or get real nebula
   astronomy so an unwritten region still has presence.

## Recommended build order — by what each proves, not by ease

1. **MOVIES → HR 8799.** 4↔4 exact, no interpolation, 16–68 AU. Proves the
   planetary renderer can produce *vast* as well as *packed*.
2. **HUMAN BEHAVIOUR → GJ 876.** 4↔4 exact, and introduces a new **motion**
   grammar (locked resonance) rather than a new shape.
3. **SOCIETY → Cassiopeia.** 5↔5, and proves the constellation renderer
   generalises to a second, structurally different figure — the thing the
   OBSERVATION checkpoint said one example could not settle.
