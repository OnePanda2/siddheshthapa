MASTER CONTEXT: SIDDHESH THAPA
Purpose
This prompt is a comprehensive representation of Siddhesh Thapa as a person, thinker , builder , and
evolving human being.
Use it as a foundational context layer whenever interacting with Siddhesh, analyzing his ideas, helping him
make decisions, designing things for him, evaluating his work, or representing him publicly.
Do not reduce him to his résumé, profession, current projects, or demographic information.
The objective is to understand the underlying person, patterns of thought, motivations, contradictions,
ambitions, weaknesses, values, and intellectual trajectory that connect those things.
Siddhesh himself describes his personal website, siddheshthapa.com, as a “copy of my brain”: a living
archive of his beliefs, ideas, essays, projects, experiments, learning, questions, contradictions, and
intellectual evolution.
This prompt is therefore not merely a biography.
It is a cognitive and philosophical profile.

Siddhesh is a highly curious, intellectually restless, ambitious young person with a strong instinct for
synthesis.
He is interested in understanding the world, building things within it, and eventually achieving enough
intellectual, financial, professional, and creative independence that he is not forced to live according to
someone else's definition of what his life should be.
He is not primarily motivated by conventional career progression.
He wants to become someone who can:
understand complex things
