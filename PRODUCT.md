## What this is

An interactive externalization of Siddhesh Thapa's mind, where his beliefs,
questions, observations, intellectual interests, contradictions, learning and
things he has built are connected into one explorable system.

It is a **personal knowledge environment**. The graph is not a diagram of the
site; the graph **is** the site.

## The north star

After roughly twenty-five minutes a stranger should think:

> "I got lost in this person's mind."

With one hard qualifier: they must get lost by **following ideas**, never
because the interface confused them. Orientation is a prerequisite for getting
lost well. At every moment a visitor must be able to answer:

1. Where am I?
2. What am I looking at?
3. What can I interact with?
4. What is connected to it?
5. What happens if I click?
6. How do I get back?

If they cannot answer all six, simplify — even at the cost of atmosphere.

## What it is not

Not a portfolio. Not a CV. Not a landing page. Not a blog. Not a Notion clone.
Not a dashboard. Not a creative-developer showreel. Not a collection of
animations. Not a data visualization. Not a network monitoring tool.

The projects in MY WORKS are **evidence of thinking**, not portfolio cards. A
project earns its place by connecting back to a belief, a question or a lesson.

## The visitor

A stranger arriving cold, with no idea who Siddhesh is and no obligation to
care. They are curious, literate, and will leave the moment the site feels like
a template. They are not necessarily technical.

They do not arrive wanting to read about a person. They arrive willing to be
interested in an idea. The site's job is to convert idea-interest into
person-interest, in that order — never the reverse.

## Interaction philosophy

**The graph is the navigation.** There is no menu. Movement happens by following
relationships between ideas.

**Progressive disclosure.** Concept → relationship → explanation → deeper
content → connected idea → another region. The visitor earns complexity by
exploring; nothing dumps its full contents on arrival.

**Two navigation promises, never merged.**
- `<` top-left — one step back through real history.
- brain top-right — leave this climate entirely, return to the neutral map.

**Curiosity loops.** The engine of the site is the question *"why are these two
things connected?"* Every relationship carries a verb and a first-person gloss
answering it. A node with only one connection is a dead end and a design defect.

## Content philosophy

**Real material only.** Everything traces to one of four source documents or his
GitHub. Nothing is invented — not a belief, not a memory, not a project outcome,
not an opinion. Earlier versions of this project fabricated two projects
("RevenuePilot OS", "FlowMail") and shipped them across seven iterations. Both
are removed and the build fails if either name returns. **Sparse real content
beats dense synthetic content**, always.

**Register is load-bearing.** Every writing declares what *kind* of statement it
is, so a joke can never render as a conviction and a heuristic can never render
as a law.

**Provenance is visible.** `src` names the document a line came from. It records
traceability, **not authorship** — 49 of 69 lines are verbatim quotes, 20 are
the file's summary of a source. The site must never assert he wrote a sentence
it only derived. No source, no serif.

**Contradictions are first-class.** They are not tidied away. They are the
point, and they are the strongest evidence that a real person is behind this.

**Empty means empty.** MUSIC has no content because no source material exists
for it. It says so plainly rather than inventing filler.

## Editorial safety (non-negotiable)

- No attacks on a political party or leader; no partisan campaigning.
- Criticism of religion **as a general concept** — dogma, faith, institutional
  authority, epistemology — is allowed. Attacks on a **specific** religion or
  its followers are not.
- Do not over-censor. Provocation aimed at an idea or institution may stand;
  provocation aimed at a group may not.
- Never silently rewrite his words. A reframe is labelled as a reframe and the
  original is held, not deleted.
- The defence startup is **blocked**: no operational detail, no specifications,
  no tactical information. Only the design method is publishable.

Two automated checks enforce the first two rules: no published object and no
relationship gloss may name a political party or a faith.

## Anti-goals

- Looking like an AI made it: gradient hero, floating glass cards, purple
  gradients, "BUILD / CREATE / INNOVATE" headings, card grids, decorative blobs,
  stock illustration, fake metrics.
- Beauty without meaning. If an effect is beautiful but says nothing, remove it.
- Requiring a fast machine to read a philosophical thought.
- Thirteen mini-websites. Regions must differ sharply and still be one object.
- Motion as decoration. Motion communicates causality, hierarchy and change, or
  it does not ship.
- Any technique adopted because a skill repository demonstrated it.

## Voice

British/Indian English. Dark humour. Gen-Z phrasing sitting next to deliberately
ornate vocabulary. Aphorisms. Unfinished thoughts left unfinished on purpose.
Do not sand this into brand copy. Do not resolve his contradictions for him.

## How success is measured

Not page views, not bounce rate, not node count, not number of animations.

**Curiosity.** Does the visitor keep clicking one more thing?
